How to use this pack

  1. Print what looks fun — every PDF is US Letter and prints fine on A4 if you choose fit to page.
  2. Cut the invitation cards and conversation strips along the dashed lines.
  3. Keep the keeper notes and reminder sheet by the computer or in your grandkid folder.
  4. Start each call with one conversation strip, then lean on the activity ideas.

A few friendly reminders

  • Short and happy beats long and forced — quit while everyone's still smiling.
  • Mind the time zones so nobody gets a 7 a.m. surprise.
  • A missed call isn't a broken promise. Send a voice note and pick it back up next week.
